# Before Change

- [ ] Confirm the requested change and authority to make it.
- [ ] Read affected files and nearby tests/docs.
- [ ] Check decisions and open questions; do not guess unresolved business rules.
- [ ] Inspect user-owned/uncommitted changes and avoid overwriting them.
- [ ] Identify security, data, compliance, compatibility, and external-service impact.
- [ ] Define validation appropriate to risk.
- [ ] Keep the proposed change minimal and reversible.

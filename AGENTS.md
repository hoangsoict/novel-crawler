# AI Agent Entry Point

Before any task, read:

1. `.agents/PROJECT_INDEX.md`
2. `.agents/ARCHITECTURE.md`
3. `.agents/TAKEOVER.md`
4. `.agents/memory/SESSION_STATE.md`
5. `.agents/memory/DECISIONS.md`
6. Relevant files under `.agents/rules/` and `.agents/skills/`

Follow the matching workflow in `.agents/workflows/`.

Do not guess business requirements. Record uncertainty in `.agents/memory/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`.

After every task, update `.agents/memory/SESSION_STATE.md` and `.agents/memory/CHANGELOG_AI.md`. Update decisions or open questions when applicable.
